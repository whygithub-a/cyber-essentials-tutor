
# Evaluation Questionnaire: Cyber Essentials Intelligent Tutoring System Prototype

## Introduction

Thank you for taking part in this evaluation.

You will use a Cyber Essentials Intelligent Tutoring System prototype. The system is designed to support learning and formative feedback. It is not an official Cyber Essentials certification platform, does not replace a Certification Body, and does not make official compliance or certification decisions.

Please do not enter real organisational or personal information into the prototype or this questionnaire. This includes company names, employee names, usernames, passwords, credentials, IP addresses, internal system details, security configurations, customer information or any confidential data.

Your questions and assessment answers in the prototype are processed by the backend and Azure OpenAI to generate AI tutor responses and formative feedback. The prototype is designed not to store raw chat transcripts or raw assessment answer text in the application database. To support progress tracking, the system uses a random browser session ID and may store limited progress metadata, such as topic ID, question ID, score, maximum score and timestamp.

The questionnaire is anonymous. Please answer based on your experience using the prototype.

# Section 2: Task Instructions

Before completing the questionnaire, please use the Cyber Essentials Intelligent Tutoring System prototype.

Please complete the following two learning sections:

1. User Access Control
2. Malware Protection

For each section, please:

1. Open the relevant topic from the Learning Sections panel.
2. Read or skim the Cyber Essentials learning material.
3. Ask the AI Tutor at least one question about the topic.
4. Open the Assessment tab.
5. Complete all three assessment questions for that topic.
6. Read the formative feedback after each answer.
7. Review your progress indicators, such as mastery, XP or badges.

You should complete six assessment questions in total: three for User Access Control and three for Malware Protection.

The task should take approximately 20–30 minutes.

## Suggested interpretation of Q9 and Q10

The short task-response questions are not intended to produce a formal learning-outcome measure or certification judgement. They provide a lightweight indication of whether participants can identify relevant Cyber Essentials points after using the prototype.

Suggested scoring:

| Score | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 0–2 | Limited identification of relevant points |
| 3 | Partial identification of relevant points |
| 4–5 | Strong identification of relevant points |

The total score across Q9 and Q10 can be reported out of 10 if useful, but this should be described as a lightweight task-response score rather than a formal test result.
