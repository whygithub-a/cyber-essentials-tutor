# Cyber Essentials Consultation Module User Evaluation

This questionnaire was used to evaluate the Consultation module of the Cyber Essentials Intelligent Tutoring System prototype.

Participants were asked to use fictional or generalised, non-sensitive organisational information when completing the Consultation.

The questionnaire was anonymous.

---

## Participant Task

Before completing the questionnaire, please:

1.Complete the consultation questionnaire using a fictional organisation or general information.
2.Use Help me understand for at least one consultation question.
3.Submit the consultation and review the generated readiness report.
4.Review the Questions to clarify section.
5.Use Help me understand for at least one clarification question.
6.Answer at least one clarification question and regenerate the report.

---

## Section 1: Consent and Completion

### 1. Consent to participate

Participants confirmed their consent before continuing with the evaluation.

### 2. Approximately how long did it take you to complete the Consultation task and review the report?

**Response type:** Single-choice time range

## Section 2: Initial Consultation Questionnaire

For each statement, participants selected:

1. Strongly Disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neither Agree nor Disagree
4. Agree
5. Strongly Agree

### Task and Questionnaire Usability

1. It was clear what I needed to do.
2. The consultation questions were easy to understand.
3. The available answer options were appropriate.
4. The consultation was easy to navigate.
5. The consultation did not feel unnecessarily long.
6. It was clear that the Consultation provides learning and readiness support rather than an official Cyber Essentials certification decision.

---

## Section 3: "Help Me Understand"

Using the same five-point scale:

1. The explanations were easy to understand.
2. The examples helped me understand how to answer the questions.
3. The explanation of why each question matters was useful.
4. The AI follow-up answers were relevant to my questions.
5. The "Help me understand" feature increased my confidence when answering the consultation questions.

---

## Section 4: Readiness Report and Clarification Process

Using the same five-point scale:

1. The overall readiness result was easy to understand.
2. Strengths and potential gaps were presented clearly.
3. The recommended actions were practical.
4. The clarification questions were relevant.
5. The explanations provided for clarification questions were helpful.
6. The regenerated report reflected the additional information I provided.
7. The report improved my understanding of Cyber Essentials readiness.

## Notes

This questionnaire evaluated initial user perceptions of:

- consultation-question clarity;
- suitability of answer options;
- navigation;
- the "Help me understand" function;
- AI follow-up explanations;
- readiness-report clarity;
- recommended actions;
- clarification questions;
- regenerated reports;
- overall ease of use;
- overall usefulness; and
- likelihood of future use.

The Consultation module provided preliminary learning and readiness guidance only. It was not an official Cyber Essentials assessment, audit, certification decision or professional consultancy service.

Participants were instructed to use fictional or generalised information and not to provide sensitive organisational security details.
