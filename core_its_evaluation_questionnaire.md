# Evaluation Questionnaire: Cyber Essentials Intelligent Tutoring System

This questionnaire was used to evaluate the core Intelligent Tutoring System (ITS), including the learning material, AI Tutor, open-ended Assessment, formative feedback and progress features.

Participants completed activities in the User Access Control and Malware Protection sections before completing this questionnaire.

The questionnaire was anonymous.

---

## Participant Task

Before completing the questionnaire, participants were asked to:

1. Complete the User Access Control learning section.
2. Ask the AI Tutor at least one question.
3. Complete all three User Access Control assessment questions.
4. Review the formative feedback and progress information.
5. Complete the Malware Protection learning section.
6. Ask the AI Tutor at least one question.
7. Complete all three Malware Protection assessment questions.
8. Review the formative feedback and progress information.

## Notes

The questionnaire was used for an exploratory participant evaluation of a dissertation prototype.

The evaluation measured user perceptions of usefulness, ease of use, clarity, trust, engagement and practical relevance. The post-task questions were used as lightweight indicators of participants' ability to identify relevant Cyber Essentials control points.

The questionnaire was not designed to demonstrate long-term learning gain or certification readiness.
